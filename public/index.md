## Services

- **AI Agents & Assistants** — Custom AI agent setup, LLM integration, and workflow automation that augment your team, not replace it. Scoped from the workflow out, with human approval gates and an exception path.
- **Web & App Development** — Full-stack web apps, responsive marketing sites, and progressive web experiences built performance-first, including Next.js static builds, e-commerce and CMS integrations.
- **Product Design (UI/UX)** — Research, information architecture, interface design, and design systems delivered as Figma specs a developer can build from without guessing.
- **QA & Maintenance** — Manual and automated testing, regression suites, accessibility and performance audits, and ongoing maintenance for sites and apps already in market.
- **VR & Immersive Tech** — WebGL and three.js experiences, 3D product visualisation, and headset-ready immersive builds for brands that need something physical-feeling.
- **Technical Strategy** — Architecture decisions, stack selection, delivery planning, and the audit work that tells a founder what to build first and what to leave alone.
